Releases and legal considerations

FeurTube distributes patch source and local build instructions. It does not offer a prepatched YouTube APK.

Research checked October 29, 2026. This explains the distribution decision; it is not a legal opinion or clearance.

Source releases

GitHub releases are intended for versioned source snapshots and compatibility notes. A source release does not contain YouTube’s APK, signing keys, or account data. Use the build guide with your own supported APK.

FeurTube’s code is GPL-3.0-only. Preserve its license and upstream notices. That license does not grant rights to Google’s proprietary app. Shipping compiled upstream patches would also require satisfying their corresponding-source obligations.

United States

Google could send a takedown demand or bring a claim; open source is not immunity. In March 2024, Google submitted a DMCA notice targeting redistributed modified YouTube binaries, including a ReVanced Extended Android module. A notice is a claim, not a court judgment, but it is a concrete distribution risk.

YouTube’s terms restrict modification, distribution and reverse engineering of its software, subject to permission and applicable law. Contract issues are separate from copyright infringement.

17 USC §1201 also regulates bypassing certain technological protections and distributing circumvention tools. Whether particular patch hooks fall within it is fact-specific. Avoiding APK redistribution removes that particular copying step; it does not settle terms or anti-circumvention questions.

Qatar

Moving to Qatar does not establish that worldwide distribution is permitted. Qatar’s Law No. 7 of 2002 protects reproduction and transformation rights (Article 7). Article 51 addresses certain devices that defeat reproduction protections and unauthorized changes to rights-management data. Applying those provisions to FeurTube requires local advice.

Before distributing modified APKs or monetizing the project, obtain advice that covers the actual patch behavior, US and Qatar law, and the intended distribution countries.

Branding

FeurTube uses its own mark and identifies itself as unofficial. This is not trademark clearance: the USPTO explains that confusing users about a product’s source or sponsorship can support an infringement claim. Do not present FeurTube as approved by Google or YouTube.

FeurTube is independent of Google and YouTube.
